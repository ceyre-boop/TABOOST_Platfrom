How Battle Scheduler works

Post your hours, find a match, lock it in. Under 30 seconds.

  1. Post your hours. Tap the nights and times you can go live under My hours.
  2. Swipe. Right to invite someone free at the same time, left to skip. Open calls book instantly.
  3. They accept. Invites land in Requests. Accepting sets the battle right away.
  4. Go live. You get reminders 60 and 15 minutes before. Start the battle in TikTok at the time.
  5. Submit your score. Open the battle in Battles and enter your final score. Both sides must agree within 5%.
